Rules for Ordering in an Online Shop
8. A valid shipping address is required for every physical delivery; providing an independent alternative billing address is optional when standard billing applies.
1. A customer must log in and receive login confirmation from the system before selecting products.
2. If the login is not successful, the shopping experience stops immediately.
3. Each selected product must be added to the shopping cart and saved before the next product is chosen.
4. The customer must check that every desired product has been selected before finishing the order.
5. Payment and shipment preparation must run simultaneously after the order is finished by the system.
6. The customer must enter valid payment data and wait for the bank to confirm the payment.
7. Payment must be confirmed by the bank within 10 minutes of submission.
8. A shipping address is required for every order.
9. An independent billing address may be entered if it differs from the shipping address.
10. The order is finished only after both the address step and the payment step are executed.
11. Payment data that fails validation must be corrected and resubmitted.
12. A customer session expires after 30 minutes of inactivity and requires a new login.
13. Only products showing an in-stock status may be added to the shopping cart.
14. The cart may be edited until the bank confirms the payment.
15. The order confirmation is sent to the customer only after payment and address steps are completed.
