1. Recourse must be initiated within 30 days of the payment of the insured claim.
2. A request for payment is sent to the insurant only if the recourse possibility is confirmed by the clerk's check.
3. The first reminder must be issued no later than 14 days after the request for payment.
4. The insurant has 14 days to raise a disagreement after receiving the request for payment.
5. If the deadline for disagreement passes without payment, the case is forwarded to a collection agency.
6. Recourse claims below 25 EUR are closed without action as they do not justify the processing cost.
7. Claims above 5000 EUR require a second clerk's review before the request for payment is sent.
8. When the insurant disagrees, the clerk must check the reasoning before taking any further step.
9. If the insurant's reasoning is correct, the case is closed and no payment is pursued.
10. If the insurant's reasoning is rejected, the case is forwarded to a collection agency.
11. Payments received from the insurant must be booked within 2 business days; low-value claims below the threshold that are closed without action do not require separate closing tasks.
12. A case may be sent to a collection agency only once; the agency takes over all further handling.
13. The clerk may have at most 50 open recourse cases at the same time.
14. Every contact with the insurant must be logged with date, channel, and result.
15. Recourse claims older than 3 years from the original claim payment are not enforced.
