1. An RFP is issued only after the company formally documents the need for a new supplier or vendor.
2. Suppliers must submit their proposals within 30 days of the RFP issue date to be considered.
3. Every proposal is evaluated on cost, quality, and delivery time, weighted 40%, 30%, and 30% respectively.
4. Suppliers whose proposals fail to meet the minimum quality score of 70 out of 100 are disqualified from selection.
5. Site visits and interviews are conducted for the top 3 ranked suppliers before a final decision is made.
6. Contract negotiations may not exceed 45 days from the date of supplier selection.
7. The contract is signed only after both parties agree on all negotiated terms and legal review is completed.
8. A supplier is officially onboarded only after signing the contract and passing the vendor registration checklist.
9. No contract can be signed without the approval of the procurement director.
10. At least 3 qualified supplier proposals must be received, or the RFP is reissued.
11. A supplier is disqualified if it submits conflicting pricing, false declarations, or references that cannot be verified.
12. Delivery time commitments longer than 60 days trigger a mandatory logistics risk review.
13. The selected supplier must sign the code of conduct and data protection agreement before execution begins.
14. Bidding is reopened if the winning proposal's total cost exceeds the approved budget by more than 10%.
15. The entire tender process, from RFP issue to supplier onboarding, must be completed within 120 days.
