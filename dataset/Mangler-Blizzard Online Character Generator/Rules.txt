1. A battle.net account is required before the character creation process can continue.
2. A WoW subscription must be verified as active before character creation, which may be established during initial account login without requiring a separate in-flow re-check task.
3. A new account is finalized only after the confirmation link in the confirmation mail has been clicked.
4. Credit card information is entered only if credit card is selected as the payment method.
5. Bank account information requires both IBAN and BIC to be entered and validated.
6. A payment method may not be left unset; the account stays blocked until a method is chosen.
7. Character names are entered one by one until a name that is still available is confirmed.
8. A chosen name is reserved for the character once the availability check passes.
9. Names rejected as unavailable cannot be retried until 24 hours have passed.
10. Realm, race, and class must be selected before the name confirmation is executed.
11. A character name may contain no more than 12 characters.
12. Each realm can host no more than 5000 characters per account region cap.
13. The player needs to come up with at least 5 candidate names before entering them into the system.
14. The confirmation with the character selfies is sent immediately after the name is reserved.
15. The player receives an additional message when the expansion is released.
