1. A course can be registered for only if at least one free slot is available.
2. The system lists only courses that still have free slots, together with their dates.
3. Course registration requires a valid user account at the sports institute.
4. New accounts may be created only by applicants who are registered at an eligible university.
5. Applicants not registered at an eligible university must request an activation and wait for the response.
6. An account becomes active only after the sports institute approves the activation request.
7. Registration can be completed only after the user has logged in successfully.
8. A user may hold a course slot for at most one course per course date.
9. Registration is finalized only after valid payment information has been provided.
10. Payment must be completed within 48 hours of reserving a slot.
11. Unpaid slot reservations are automatically cancelled after 48 hours and the slot is released.
12. A course ticket is issued only after the payment has been confirmed.
13. If the user chooses to tweet course registration to friends, Twitter authorization is required; social media sharing is entirely optional and not required for course registration.
14. Account creation is rejected if the applicant's university is not in the list of eligible universities.
15. A slot is released back to the course pool when a registration is cancelled or payment fails.
