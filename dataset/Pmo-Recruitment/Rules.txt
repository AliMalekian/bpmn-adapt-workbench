Rules for the Recruitment and Onboarding Process
10. When salary discussions occur, the agreed salary must stay within the approved budget band; candidates accepting standard scale offers do not undergo salary negotiations.
1. A hiring request must be approved by the department head before HR creates a job description.
2. HR must post the job description on at least 3 job boards.
3. Resumes are accepted until the application deadline, 14 days after the job is posted.
4. All collected resumes must be screened against the required qualifications for the role.
5. Only candidates passing the resume screening are invited to an initial phone interview.
6. Candidates who pass the phone interview are invited to an in-person or virtual interview.
7. The interview format must follow the candidate's stated preference.
8. Each interview panel must include at least 3 members.
9. The chosen candidate must pass reference checks before an offer is extended.
10. Salary negotiations are allowed but the final salary must stay within the approved budget band.
11. Onboarding begins only after the candidate formally accepts the offer.
12. Onboarding must include paperwork, orientation, and role-specific training.
13. The process ends once all onboarding steps are completed and the new hire is integrated into the team.
14. The entire hiring process must be completed within 60 days of the approved hiring request.
15. Every onboarding step must be marked complete in the HR system before the process is closed.
