1. A service request is accepted only if it includes a description of the symptoms and the exact fridge type.
2. The central service center assigns each request to the nearest local service facility that has availability within 48 hours.
3. Every service visit must be confirmed by the customer at least 24 hours before the technician's arrival window.
4. If the technician cannot complete the repair in one visit, a parts work order must be created before leaving the customer's home.
5. Follow-up visits for missing parts must be scheduled within 5 working days of the initial visit.
6. A repair is considered complete only after the customer confirms that the fridge is working correctly again.
7. A service facility can be rated by the customer only after a successful completion confirmation.
8. Service facilities whose average customer rating falls below 3.5 out of 5 over a quarter are placed under central review.
9. Emergency repair requests must be scheduled within 24 hours, while regular requests may be scheduled within 7 days.
10. No more than 3 follow-up visits are allowed per service request without approval from the central service center.
11. Replacement parts must be ordered by the service facility within 2 working days of identifying the need.
12. A service request is automatically closed if the customer cannot be reached for confirmation within 30 days.
13. Customer ratings of 2 stars or lower trigger a mandatory quality review by the central service center.
14. Warranty repairs are covered only for fridge models on the manufacturer's supported model list.
15. Each local service facility must keep a minimum stock of the 20 most frequently requested fridge parts.
