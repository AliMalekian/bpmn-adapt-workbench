1. Invitations are sent to exactly 10 friends before the party planning continues.
2. A date for the LAN party is appointed only after the list of wanted games is received.
3. At least 8 of the 10 friends must agree on a date for the party to be confirmed.
4. If fewer than 8 friends agree, another date is appointed until 8 friends are free.
5. While a date is being found, the host checks whether all wanted games are available.
6. Missing games are downloaded before the party date.
7. The host must find out how much beer the friends require before the party.
8. If the available beer stock is lower than the required amount, the missing quantity is bought.
9. Every participant must have their own seat and network port at the LAN party.
10. A game may be played only if at least 2 participants have installed it.
11. The party cannot start until all promised games are downloaded and installed on the LAN network.
12. At least the required beer amount, rounded up to full crates, must be in stock at party start.
13. Date changes are communicated to all 10 friends within 24 hours.
14. The party is postponed if fewer than 5 friends are available on the final confirmed date.
15. The chosen date must not fall on a day when more than 3 friends are unavailable.
