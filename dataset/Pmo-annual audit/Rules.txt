1. The audit coordinator starts the annual audit by sending a notification to every regional office.
2. The regional office must submit its financial statements and supporting documents within 30 days of the notification.
3. The compliance team must check for regulatory updates that affect the audit before document review begins.
4. The central audit team reviews the submission and requests clarifications if any discrepancies are found.
5. The regional office must provide the requested clarifications within 10 working days.
6. The risk assessment evaluates financial, operational, and compliance risks, and all three must be completed.
7. A detailed investigation, including data analysis and interviews, is launched when high risk is identified.
8. Site visits are performed for high-risk findings when data analysis and interviews are insufficient.
9. The audit report is compiled only after all identified risks have been addressed.
10. The audit director must review the report and may approve it or send it back for revisions.
11. If revisions are required, the audit team updates the report and resubmits it for a new review cycle.
12. A report may be returned for revisions no more than 3 times before it is escalated to the executive board.
13. The final report is distributed to the executive board and archived once approved.
14. The entire audit process is closed only after the final report is distributed and archived.
15. All audit working papers and evidence must be retained for at least 7 years after the audit closes.
