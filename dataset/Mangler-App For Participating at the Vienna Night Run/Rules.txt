1. A runner must select a starting block before the run attempt can be registered in the app.
2. The official 5 km time must be below 25 minutes before a starting number is issued.
3. Runners who fail the 25-minute target must train and recheck until the goal is achieved.
4. Starting numbers are issued only after the qualifying time is confirmed by the fitness tracking gadget.
5. If more than 60 minutes remain between the end of the workday and the run start time, the runner may travel from home; otherwise they must leave directly from work.
6. The app asks for the runner's workday end time to compute the departure decision.
7. Times measured by fitness gadgets override manually entered times for the qualification check.
8. Registration closes 24 hours before the event start.
9. Each starting block is limited to 500 runners.
10. The official final running time is measured from the start signal to the finish line crossing.
11. Runners must complete the 5 km track within 90 minutes to be ranked with a final time.
12. The app must display the final running time within 15 minutes after the runner finishes.
13. Runners must use at least one drinking station during the run because they run and drink at the same time.
14. The selected starting block cannot be changed after the starting number is issued.
15. Qualification and final time data are synchronized with the fitness gadget before and after the run.
