1. Invitations must be sent to exactly 10 friends before planning can continue.
2. The host collects the preferred games from all invited friends before a date is proposed.
3. A LAN party date is confirmed only when 8 or more of the 10 friends agree on that date.
4. If fewer than 8 friends agree on the proposed date, another date must be appointed and voted on again.
5. Games requested by the guests must be available; if any game is missing, it must be downloaded before the party.
6. Beer consumption is estimated for the confirmed attendees, and missing beer must be bought before the party.
7. A friend who has not replied 48 hours after the invitation is treated as unavailable for the current date vote.
8. A maximum of 2 date re-votes is allowed before the party is scheduled on the date with the most votes.
9. Only games with valid licenses may be downloaded and used at the LAN party.
10. The download of a missing game must be finished at least 24 hours before the party starts.
11. No alcoholic beverages may be served to guests under 18 years of age, where applicable by law.
12. The venue must provide at least one spare network port per 2 guests.
13. Beer stock must cover at least 2 bottles per hour per attending guest for the full planned duration.
14. The party may last at most 10 hours of active playing time per day.
15. If fewer than 8 friends are available for any proposed date, no party can take place on that date.
