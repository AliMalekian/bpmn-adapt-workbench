Rules for Building a Custom Machine out of Lego Bricks
14. When assembled as a custom prototype, the machine must pass an integration test; subcomponents assembled for standalone module delivery do not require full machine assembly.
1. A basic machine design must be fully developed and approved before any Lego brick sets are ordered.
2. Lego brick sets must be ordered only from the list of approved brick set codes.
3. The sorting group must sort all parts into clearly labeled containers before any subcomponent is built.
4. Each subcomponent must be built individually using parts taken only from the sorted containers.
5. If a container runs out of parts, individual replacement parts must be reordered immediately and the container refilled before assembly continues.
6. Subcomponents may only be built from parts that match the design specification; mismatched parts are returned to the sorting group.
7. Brickset delivery must be completed within 7 days of placing an order.
8. The sorting group must be supervised by at least one adult supervisor per 10 children.
9. Each subcomponent must be tested individually after it is built, before it is assembled.
10. Any subcomponent that fails its test must be redesigned and rebuilt before it is tested again.
11. Assembly of the machine may begin only after every subcomponent has passed its individual test.
12. A maximum of 3 failed tests is allowed per subcomponent before the design must be redone from scratch.
13. Reorders of individual parts must be placed within 24 hours of a container becoming empty.
14. The complete machine must pass a final integration test before it is accepted as finished.
15. All finished machines must be dismantled and their parts sorted back into containers unless stored as an approved model.
