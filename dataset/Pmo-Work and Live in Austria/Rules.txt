1. A visa application is accepted only if the application form and all required supporting documents are complete.
2. The travel document must be valid in Austria for at least three months beyond the visa duration and contain at least two empty pages.
3. The travel document must have been issued within the last ten years.
4. A passport photo of 35 x 45 mm must comply with the specified Austrian passport photo criteria.
5. Fully comprehensive travel health insurance is required for the planned stay, with a minimum cover of 30,000 Euro valid across the entire Schengen area.
6. The applicant must prove sufficient means of subsistence for the stay and for the return journey.
7. A visa is refused if grounds for refusal exist, such as a residence prohibition or a Schengen alert.
8. A Schengen visa is issued by the representation office of the country of the main travel destination.
9. If no main travel destination can be determined, the country of first entry into the Schengen area issues the visa.
10. Territorial competence for category A and C visas is the representation office in the consular district of the applicant's legal place of residence.
11. Type D visas must be applied for at the competent Austrian representation.
12. A Rot-Weiss-Rot Card visa must be renewed every set number of months as defined by the current regulations.
13. Accommodation must be arranged and a bank account opened before the work placement begins.
14. Applicants may submit evidence such as hotel reservations, invitations, booking confirmations, return tickets, and proof of employment as required.
15. The applicant must contact the relevant representation in advance, since additional evidence requirements may vary by location.
