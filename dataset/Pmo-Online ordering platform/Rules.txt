Rules for an Online Ordering and Delivery Platform
11. When customer feedback is submitted after delivery, it is recorded in the system; submitting feedback is voluntary for the customer.
1. Orders may be placed through the online platform or by phone between 8:00 and 20:00 local time.
2. The system must automatically generate and send an order confirmation immediately after the order is placed.
3. The warehouse team must pick and pack the ordered items within 24 hours of the confirmation.
4. A shipping label is generated only after picking and packing is completed.
5. Every order must be handed over to an approved logistics provider for delivery.
6. Tracking information must be sent to the customer as soon as the shipment is dispatched.
7. The shipment must be monitored until it reaches the customer's address.
8. Delivery must be completed within 5 business days of dispatch.
9. If the first delivery attempt fails, the logistics provider must offer up to 2 redelivery attempts.
10. Orders with a total value below 5 dollars cannot be placed.
11. Customer feedback collected after delivery must be recorded in the system.
12. Returns may be initiated within 30 days of delivery.
13. Returned items are inspected and restocked only if they are undamaged.
14. Payment must be validated before the order is sent to the warehouse for picking.
15. The process ends only after delivery is completed and any confirmed return is fully resolved.
