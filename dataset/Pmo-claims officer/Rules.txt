1. A claim may be examined only after it has been registered in the claims system.
2. The claims officer must write a settlement recommendation for every claim examined within 5 business days.
3. Each settlement recommendation must state a proposed settlement amount and the justification supporting it.
4. The senior claims officer reviews every recommendation and must mark it as OK or Not OK; no other outcome is permitted.
5. A claim marked as Not OK is returned to the claims officer together with the senior officer's stated reasons.
6. The recommendation may be reworked no more than 3 times before the claim is escalated to the department manager.
7. Claims with a proposed settlement above $50,000 are subject to mandatory senior claims officer review.
8. Recommendations that exceed the policy coverage limit require written approval from the department manager.
9. All decisions and rework rounds must be documented in the claim file.
10. A claim proceeds to the handling phase only after the senior claims officer marks it as OK.
11. The senior claims officer may not approve a recommendation that he or she wrote personally.
12. Claims with incomplete documentation are returned for completion before examination begins.
13. The entire claim review must be completed within a maximum of 30 days from registration.
14. Settlement recommendations must be based exclusively on the terms of the governing insurance policy.
15. Claims older than 6 months without a decision are automatically reported to the audit office.
