Request1:
Removed element: The "Claim is sent back" task (Task_6) - the Not OK rework loop that returns the claim to the claims officer so the settlement recommendation can be repeated.
Reason: It is proposed for removal to speed up claim handling by eliminating the repeat/rework cycle for recommendations that were not accepted the first time.
Conflict details: This removal CONFLICTS with Rule 5 of Rules.txt: "A claim marked as Not OK is returned to the claims officer together with the senior officer's stated reasons," and with Rule 6: "The recommendation may be reworked no more than 3 times before the claim is escalated to the department manager." Both rules presuppose that a Not OK claim is sent back to the claims officer for rework. Removing the send-back task removes the whole rework mechanism those rules govern, so the removal is not executable without breaking Rules 5 and 6.

Request2:
Removed element: The "Recommendation is checked by a senior claims officer" task (Task_4) - the senior review of each settlement recommendation.
Reason: It is proposed for removal to reduce the senior officers' workload and to let claims move to the handling phase after only the claims officer's assessment.
Conflict details: This removal CONFLICTS with the resources in Resources.txt. The resource "Senior claims officers: 2" is allocated precisely to perform this senior review; "Senior review turnaround (business days): 3" defines the time the resource is budgeted to spend on this step; and "Settlement threshold requiring senior review ($): 50,000" determines when this review must engage. Removing the senior check leaves the two allocated senior claims officers and their 3-day review turnaround without their designated use, so the removal contradicts the resource plan of the process.

Request3:
Removed element: The "Claims officer writes a settlement recommendation" task (Task_3) - writing the settlement recommendation with amount and justification.
Reason: It is proposed for removal so that the claim advances immediately from the examination to the senior officer's marking, saving the claims officers' writing time.
Conflict details: This removal CONFLICTS with both rules and resources. Rules: Rule 2 of Rules.txt states that "The claims officer must write a settlement recommendation for every claim examined within 5 business days," Rule 3 requires "Each settlement recommendation... to state a proposed settlement amount and the justification supporting it," and Rule 14 requires recommendations to be "based exclusively on the terms of the governing insurance policy." All three rules presuppose that a written recommendation is produced. Resources: the resource "Claims officers: 6" and "Maximum claims per officer per day: 8" in Resources.txt allocate officer capacity specifically for examining claims and producing settlement recommendations, and "Claims examination deadline (business days): 5" budgets the time window in which the recommendation must be written; removing the writing task leaves this allocated officer capacity without its designated purpose.
