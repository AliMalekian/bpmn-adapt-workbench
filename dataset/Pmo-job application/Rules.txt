1. A job seeker must report the companies they wrote applications to, on a regular basis, at least once every week.
2. Applications that are not confirmed as received by the company within 14 days are marked as pending and automatically followed up.
3. New job offers are suggested to a job seeker only based on the companies and fields of their previously submitted applications.
4. A company must confirm receipt and rate a job application within 30 days of receiving it.
5. A job interview may be negotiated only after the company has confirmed receipt and rated the application.
6. A job seeker enters the probation phase only when a company formally offers them a job.
7. A probation phase lasts at least 1 month and at most 6 months before it is finalized.
8. After the probation phase, the job seeker and the company may rate each other within 7 days.
9. Reviews written by a company can be seen by job applicants only after 1 year has passed.
10. If the job becomes permanent, the process ends and the job seeker stops receiving job offers.
11. If the job becomes permanent but the company is rated C or lower, the job seeker continues to receive job offers.
12. A job seeker in a permanent position with a rating of C or lower no longer has to report their applications.
13. A job seeker may retract a submitted application at any time before the company confirms receipt.
14. Discrepancies between reported and actually sent applications above 2 per month are flagged for review.
15. Only job offers from companies with an average rating of B or higher are shown to job seekers with a rating of A.
