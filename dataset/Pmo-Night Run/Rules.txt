Rules for Participating in the Vienna Night Run App

1. A participant must select a starting block before registering for the Vienna Night Run.
2. A qualifying run of 5 km must be completed in less than 25 minutes to qualify for the event.
3. Participants who fail to qualify must train further and recheck their time until the goal is achieved.
4. A starting number is issued only after the qualification goal has been achieved.
5. The participant's fitness gadget must be paired with the app before running time is measured.
6. Running time must be measured automatically by the fitness gadget, not entered manually.
7. The end of the participant's workday must be entered into the app to plan travel to the run.
8. If more than one hour remains between the end of the workday and the start time, the participant may travel from home.
9. If one hour or less remains between the end of the workday and the start time, the participant must leave directly from work.
10. Each participant must stop at every designated drink station along the 5 km route.
11. The starting number must be shown at the gate to enter the assigned starting block.
12. The final running time is issued and saved to the app when the participant crosses the finish line.
13. Registration for the event closes 48 hours before the start time.
14. Participants must be at their starting block at least 30 minutes before the start.
15. The measured time is valid only if the participant completes the full 5 km route.
