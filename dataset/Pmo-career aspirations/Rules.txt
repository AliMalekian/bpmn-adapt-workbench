1. A personal development plan is created only after an employee's development needs or career aspirations have been explicitly identified and documented.
2. Every personal development plan must include at least 3 measurable milestones with target completion dates.
3. Training programs included in the plan require approval of both the manager and the HR department before enrollment.
4. A mentor is assigned only to employees accepted into the mentorship track, and the mentor must be at least two grade levels above the employee.
5. An employee is considered for promotion only after completing at least 80% of the milestones defined in the development plan.
6. Additional responsibilities are granted on a temporary basis and are withdrawn if the formal performance review outcome is unsuccessful.
7. Supervisors must provide formal feedback at least once every quarter during the development period.
8. The formal performance review is conducted by HR only after the supervisor confirms that the required milestones have been reached.
9. HR must complete its review of a promotion proposal within 30 days of submission.
10. An employee whose promotion is rejected must wait at least 6 months before being reconsidered for the same role.
11. A promotion is finalized only after the compensation adjustment is approved by both HR and the finance department.
12. Employees still within their probation period are not eligible for promotion.
13. The new role takes effect no later than 30 days after the promotion is approved.
14. All development activities completed by the employee must be documented and attached to the final performance review file.
15. No employee may be promoted into a role for which they have not completed the mandatory training specified in the development plan.
