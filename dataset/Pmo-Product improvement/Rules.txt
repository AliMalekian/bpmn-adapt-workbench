Rules for Product Improvement and Prototype Development
14. When active monthly steering reviews are scheduled, the R&D team reports progress to the committee; administrative progress reports are not mandatory during autonomous sprint cycles.
1. Every product improvement idea must be registered and documented before R&D work begins.
2. R&D must conduct initial research and feasibility studies on every registered idea.
3. At least two design concepts must be drafted for each idea that passes the feasibility study.
4. A design is selected based on its feasibility, cost, and market potential scores.
5. Prototypes must be built using only available, approved materials and resources.
6. Each prototype must undergo functionality, safety, and market potential tests.
7. All feedback from the testing phase must be collected and documented.
8. A prototype that fails a test must be refined and the testing phase reinitiated.
9. A prototype may be refined a maximum of 3 times before it must be discarded.
10. Safety tests must be conducted in the dedicated testing laboratory.
11. Prototype material costs must not exceed 40 percent of the total project budget.
12. A prototype may be approved for further development only by the project manager.
13. Only prototypes that pass all tests are recommended for further development.
14. The R&D team must report progress to the steering committee once per month.
15. The process ends when the prototype is either approved for further development or discarded.
