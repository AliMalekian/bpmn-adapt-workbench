1. When a customer places an order, the system must record the full order details before payment processing begins.
2. If payment fails, the customer is notified within 24 hours and the order is cancelled.
3. A payment is considered failed after 3 unsuccessful processing attempts.
4. After successful payment, the inventory system must check stock availability for every ordered item.
5. If any item is out of stock, a back-order is initiated and the customer is informed about the expected delay.
6. The order processing continues only when all back-ordered items have been received and are in stock.
7. In-stock items are picked from the warehouse within 1 business day of payment confirmation.
8. Quality control must check every picked item before it is moved to the packaging stage.
9. Gift wrapping is added only if the customer explicitly requested it at the time of the order.
10. International orders require customs documentation to be prepared before dispatch.
11. An order may be dispatched only when both the packaging and the shipping documents are ready.
12. A shipping confirmation is sent to the customer on the day the order is dispatched.
13. Inventory levels are updated within the same day after the order is dispatched.
14. A follow-up feedback email is sent only after the customer confirms successful delivery.
15. If the customer reports an issue upon delivery, a return label is issued and the return must be processed within 10 business days.
