1. The requirements are collected and sent to the architect before any plan is created.
2. The draft is refined multiple times with additional requirements until the builder is satisfied.
3. The list of needed materials is created only from the final approved plan.
4. The materials are ordered from several online stores, grouped by category.
5. Friends are messaged about building the house while the material order is still being processed.
6. The building work starts only after the ordered materials have arrived.
7. Invitations for the tree house party are sent only after the house has been built.
8. A list of people attending the party is created before buying the snacks.
9. Snacks are bought only for the people on the confirmed attendance list.
10. When planning an informal gathering, friend confirmations help estimate catering; formal guest list creation is optional before buying event refreshments.
11. Each material category is ordered from a separate online store.
12. Building cannot start before the plan is finalized.
13. Additional requirements cannot be changed after the material order has been placed.
14. Every invitation carries the maximum number of guests allowed.
15. The snack budget must not exceed the allocated party budget.
