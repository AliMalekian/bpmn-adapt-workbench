1. All web shops offering a required part must be queried for price, available quantity, and delivery time before any order is placed.
2. When ordering from multiple external web shops, multi-shop orders are placed only if a single shop cannot fulfill the quantity; consolidated inventory orders do not require formal shop list creation.
3. The cheapest combined offer is always chosen when its delivery time is 10 days or less.
4. When a single shop can deliver fewer than 10 units of a requested part, the remaining quantity is ordered from a second shop.
5. Parts arrive in batches and may arrive up to 2 days earlier or later than the promised delivery date.
6. Assembly may begin as soon as the first arriving parts for the project pass inspection.
7. If the stock of a single part falls below 5 units, a reorder using the cheapest offer is triggered automatically.
8. If the stock of a single part falls below 3 units, an express reorder with the fastest delivery is triggered.
9. When the stock of any part reaches zero, no further building that uses that part is allowed until the reorder arrives.
10. At least 2 different web shops must be available as suppliers for every part type in the project.
11. A complaint mail to friends is sent only when a part's stock drops to zero during active production.
12. The total cost of all parts for a project must not exceed the project's parts budget.
13. Delivery delays of more than 3 days beyond the expected date must be reported in the project log.
14. Every reordered batch must be counted and inspected before it is added to stock.
15. Two quotes are compared only when both shops list the part and both can deliver within the requested delivery window.
