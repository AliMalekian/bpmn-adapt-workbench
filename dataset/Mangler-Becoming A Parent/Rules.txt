1. Maternity leave can be planned, taken, and extended by either parent according to the selected model.
2. The parent must select a maternity leave model when the planning process starts.
3. The duration of the leave must be configured in full months and split explicitly between the parents in shared models.
4. Social Security must be notified no later than 12 weeks before the expected leave start.
5. The employer must be notified at least 8 weeks before the leave begins.
6. A change of model is only possible within the first 2 weeks of the leave.
7. The parent decides about an extension no earlier than 3 months before the original leave period ends.
8. An extension may not exceed 6 additional months and must not exceed 24 months in total.
9. Employer response to the leave notification must be acknowledged within 14 days of sending.
10. Company-provided documents submitted to Social Security may not be older than 30 days at the time of filing.
11. The process cannot create an overlap in which both parents are on leave at the same time in an exclusive model.
12. Requests for a retroactive extension older than 30 days after the leave end are rejected.
13. Every update to the leave plan must be communicated to both Social Security and the employer within 5 business days.
14. Leave costs for the employer may not exceed 40% of the employee's average monthly salary as co-payment.
15. The platform must verify the parental status of the applicant before any leave is granted.
