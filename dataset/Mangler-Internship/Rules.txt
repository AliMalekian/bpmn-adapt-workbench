1. When registering on the platform, an interest topic and desired salary are entered; optional profile sections like hobbies and secondary experiences may be omitted without restriction.
2. Experience and hobbies are optional but recommended to increase the chance of matching offers.
3. Offers arrive at arbitrary points in time and each offer lists a company, topic, and salary.
4. At most one internship offer may be accepted by the applicant.
5. As soon as one offer is accepted, all other pending offers become invalid automatically.
6. The applicant must write at least 3 status updates about the internship every week.
7. The company must write at least 3 status updates about the applicant every week.
8. An offer can be accepted or denied within 48 hours of receipt; after that the offer may be withdrawn.
9. An accepted offer may be denied later only if the company fails to publish 3 weekly updates for 2 consecutive weeks.
10. After the internship ends, the applicant may recommend the company to a list of friends.
11. Recommendations are published as separate tweets sent in parallel on twitter.
12. The internship lasts a minimum of 3 months and a maximum of 12 months.
13. The desired salary must be within the platform's range of 1,000 to 3,000 units per month.
14. Only completed internships allow company recommendations to be posted.
15. The applicant must keep at least one unanswered offer open until an acceptance is confirmed.
