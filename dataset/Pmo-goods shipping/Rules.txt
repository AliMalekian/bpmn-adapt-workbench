1. Shipping begins only after the secretary determines who will perform the actual shipping of the goods.
2. Shipments above a total weight of 30 kg are classified as large amounts and require special shipping.
3. For large amounts, the secretary must invite exactly three logistic companies to submit offers.
4. The secretary selects one logistic company from the submitted offers based on the best combination of price and delivery time.
5. A package label must be written by the secretary for every shipment before it is handed over.
6. Parcel insurance is taken by the logistics department head whenever the shipment value exceeds $500.
7. Goods must be fully packaged by the warehousemen before the logistic company picks them up.
8. Small amounts up to 30 kg are shipped using normal post shipment.
9. Packaged goods are prepared for pickup only after the label and any required insurance are in place.
10. Offers from logistic companies remain valid for a maximum of 7 days after submission.
11. The chosen logistic company must be notified of the pickup at least 1 day in advance.
12. Unsafe or damaged packaging must be redone by the warehousemen before pickup is authorized.
13. Normal post shipments are dispatched at the end of each working day.
14. The secretary must keep a record of offers, selection, and insurance for every special shipment.
15. The logistics department head approves the parcel insurance decision for every shipment above the value threshold.
