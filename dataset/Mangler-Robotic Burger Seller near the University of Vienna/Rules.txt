1. The robot accepts only one order at a time.
2. The customer must first decide whether they want a menu or only the burger.
3. If a menu is ordered, the drink preparation starts immediately.
4. The drink preparation time varies depending on the selected drink size.
5. While the drink is being prepared, the robot asks whether the customer wants fries or wedges.
6. If fries are ordered, the robot prepares fries; if wedges are ordered, it prepares wedges.
7. The fries and wedges preparation time varies with the ordered size.
8. The burger is prepared only after the side dish decision has been made.
9. The robot gives the customer an enthusiastic status update every 30 seconds while the burger is prepared.
10. The burger preparation time varies with the number of ingredients ordered.
11. The order is delivered exclusively via the conveyor belt.
12. An order is handed over when preparation is complete; live preparation status display to the customer is an optional service enhancement.
13. The customer may not change the menu type after preparation has started.
14. Orders containing items that are not on the menu are rejected by the robot.
15. The delivery is released on the conveyor belt only after the whole order is finished.
