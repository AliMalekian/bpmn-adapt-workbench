1. An external compliance audit may be scheduled only after the company has submitted the required audit application and fee.
2. The internal self-assessment must cover all mandatory clauses of the applicable standard before external auditors are invited.
3. All supporting evidence and documentation must be collected and organized no later than 10 days before the external audit begins.
4. Every identified gap must be assigned to a responsible owner with a corrective action deadline.
5. Corrective actions for high-risk findings must be completed within 30 days; low-risk findings within 60 days.
6. The final audit may proceed only after all high-priority nonconformities have been corrected and verified.
7. Certification is awarded only if all mandatory criteria and at least 90% of all audit criteria are met.
8. A certification audit team must include at least one auditor independent of the company being audited.
9. Official certification documents are issued only after the certification committee approves the audit report.
10. Certification is valid for 3 years and requires an annual surveillance audit to be maintained.
11. Temporary corrective actions are permitted only if the auditor approves the proposed deadline for full compliance.
12. Audit findings with health or safety impact are escalated to management within 24 hours of discovery.
13. The certification decision must be documented and issued no later than 30 days after the final audit closes.
14. Nonconformities that remain open after the allowed deadline cause the certification to be withheld.
15. Any staff member involved in preparing the audited processes may not serve on the internal audit team for the same scope.
