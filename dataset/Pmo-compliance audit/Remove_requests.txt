Request1:
Removed element: The "Identify gaps or issues" task (Task_7) - the examination step in which the external auditors review the company's processes and identify any gaps or issues.
Reason: It is proposed for removal to compress the audit timeline, assuming the internal self-assessment already knows the nonconformities so a separate auditor gap-identification step is no longer needed.
Conflict details: This removal CONFLICTS with Rule 4 of Rules.txt: "Every identified gap must be assigned to a responsible owner with a corrective action deadline," and with Rule 5: "Corrective actions for high-risk findings must be completed within 30 days; low-risk findings within 60 days." Both rules presuppose that gaps are identified during the audit so they can be owned, prioritized, and corrected. Removing the gap-identification step makes it impossible to assign gap owners or apply the high/low-risk corrective-action deadlines, directly breaking Rules 4 and 5. Rule 6 also fails, since "all high-priority nonconformities" cannot be corrected if no identification step exists.

Request2:
Removed element: The "Gather evidence" task (Task_9) - the internal team's activity of collecting supporting evidence for the standard's clauses during the preparation phase.
Reason: It is proposed for removal to cut preparation effort, relying only on the documentation that is already prepared by the team.
Conflict details: This removal CONFLICTS with the resources in Resources.txt. Rule 3 of Rules.txt requires "all supporting evidence and documentation [to be] collected and organized no later than 10 days before the external audit begins," and Resources.txt explicitly provisions "Documentation preparation deadline before audit (days): 10" - the allocated preparation window that is budgeted for gathering both documentation and evidence. By removing the evidence-gathering activity, the provisioned 10-day preparation window is left without its evidence-collection purpose, and a central deliverable of the internal preparation phase is lost; the removal therefore contradicts the resource plan that the process relies on.

Request3:
Removed element: The "Conduct final audit" task (Task_10) - the final external audit performed after the necessary corrections or improvements have been made.
Reason: It is proposed for removal to save the final audit days, assuming the initial external audit already established compliance after the corrections.
Conflict details: This removal CONFLICTS with both rules and resources. Rules: Rule 6 of Rules.txt states that "The final audit may proceed only after all high-priority nonconformities have been corrected and verified," Rule 7 requires certification criteria to be checked against "at least 90% of all audit criteria," and Rule 13 requires "The certification decision... no later than 30 days after the final audit closes" - all of which presuppose a final audit that verifies the corrections and closes the certification cycle. Rules 9 and 10 (certification committee approval and the 3-year validity with annual surveillance) also depend on the outcomes of the final audit. Removing the final audit breaks these rules. Resources: the resource "External audit days allocated per year: 10" in Resources.txt is partly budgeted for the final audit; "High-risk corrective action deadline (days): 30" and "Low-risk corrective action deadline (days): 60" only make sense as deadlines measured up to the final audit; and "Certification decision turnaround after audit closes (days): 30" is defined relative to the final audit. Removing the final audit contradicts these allocated audit resources and time frames.

Request4:
Removed element: The "Make necessary corrections or improvements" task (Task_2) - the corrective-actions step in which the company fixes the gaps and issues identified by the auditors, before the final audit. Note: the removal is limited to the level of the process graph, i.e., this task is dropped from the model; the described corrective improvement activity is not otherwise re-created elsewhere.
Reason: It is proposed for removal because no corrective action is needed in this particular audit cycle, and removing the task streamlines the model by letting the audit go straight from the gap-identification step into the final audit.
Conflict details: None — fully compatible with all rules and resources. Under the removal, the decision to drop the corrections task only holds when the current audit cycle has no identified gaps, and Rule 4 ("Every identified gap must be assigned to a responsible owner with a corrective action deadline") remains effective: whenever the audit does find gaps in another cycle, corrective action is still performed, so Rule 4 is not violated. Rule 5 (high-/low-risk deadlines) concerns the deadlines for corrective actions and is triggered only when corrections are actually needed, so it is also compatible. Rule 6 requires that "all high-priority nonconformities" are corrected before the final audit; with no gaps identified, there are no nonconformities to correct, so the rule is satisfied vacuously. No resource in Resources.txt mandates a fixed corrections activity: the resources "High-risk corrective action deadline (days): 30", "Low-risk corrective action deadline (days): 60", and "Current open corrective actions: 7" describe deadlines and state of open actions, and in a cycle with no identified gaps no corrective capacity is required. The final audit can then proceed directly and the process remains fully executable.

Note (for the re-add request): Request4 removes the "Make necessary corrections or improvements" task as a model element. It does not remove any gaps, findings, or audit outcomes, and no other part of the process is affected by this removal.
