1. OEE is calculated continuously from availability, performance, and quality data collected from the machine.
2. If the calculated OEE falls below 60 percent, the machine is stopped automatically.
3. When the machine is stopped because of low OEE, an email is sent to the assigned engineer.
4. The machine may be restarted only by the engineer and only after the OEE has recovered to at least 65 percent.
5. OEE measurement resumes automatically as soon as the machine is restarted.
6. The OEE process stops completely when the machine is disconnected from the system.
7. OEE is recomputed at least once every 8 hours, covering each production shift.
8. Availability excludes all idle time, wait time, and breakdown time from the scheduled production time.
9. Performance losses are counted only for the time the machine is actually running.
10. Quality losses count only the units that fail inspection.
11. The stop email to the engineer is sent only once per stop event, even if the OEE stays below the threshold for a long time.
12. No OEE data is collected while the machine is disconnected from the system.
13. An OEE alert is escalated to the maintenance supervisor if the assigned engineer does not respond within 2 hours.
14. The OEE dashboard is refreshed at least every 30 seconds during production.
15. Every stop and restart event is recorded with a timestamp in the maintenance log.
