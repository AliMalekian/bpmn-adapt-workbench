1. Department objectives must be submitted to the strategic planning office no later than 30 days before the start of the upcoming period.
2. Every objective included in the plan must be directly aligned with at least one broader organizational goal.
3. Cost estimates must be provided for every required item, including hiring, equipment, and marketing efforts.
4. Each budgeted item must be accompanied by a written justification before it is reviewed.
5. The plan is reviewed in a strategic alignment meeting before it is passed to the finance department.
6. The finance department must complete its feasibility review within 15 business days of receiving the plan.
7. A budget may undergo several review-and-adjustment rounds, each documented and approved by the necessary stakeholders.
8. Budget adjustments exceeding 10% of the original request require approval by senior management.
9. No budget is allocated until the final version is approved by the finance department.
10. Funds are distributed to the department only after the final approval is recorded.
11. Unspent funds are returned to the central budget at the end of the period.
12. The department may not begin procurement against the new budget until the allocation is confirmed in writing.
13. Hiring targets included in the plan must be approved by HR before the budget is finalized.
14. Emergency reallocation of equipment funds above $5,000 must be pre-authorized by the finance department.
15. The department must report actual spending against the approved plan on a monthly basis.
