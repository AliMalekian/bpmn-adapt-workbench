Request1:
Removed element: The "Conduct strategic alignment meeting" task (Task_10) - the review of the drafted plan in a strategic alignment meeting to ensure it aligns with broader organizational goals.
Reason: It is proposed for removal to speed up the budget-planning cycle, sending the drafted plan directly to the finance feasibility review.
Conflict details: This removal CONFLICTS with Rule 5 of Rules.txt: "The plan is reviewed in a strategic alignment meeting before it is passed to the finance department." Rule 5 makes the strategic alignment meeting a mandatory precondition before the finance department may receive the plan, so removing the meeting task directly breaks Rule 5. It also weakens Rule 2 (every objective must align with at least one broader organizational goal), because the alignment check that verifies this rule is the meeting that is being removed.

Request2:
Removed element: The "Draft plan" task (Task_6) - the step in which the team drafts the budget plan including cost estimates and justifications for each required item.
Reason: It is proposed for removal so that the outlined objectives are reviewed directly in the strategic alignment meeting without an intermediate drafting step.
Conflict details: This removal CONFLICTS with the resources in Resources.txt. The resource "Budget submission lead time before period start (days): 30" provisions a 30-day window within which the drafted plan must be submitted to the strategic planning office; and "Maximum review rounds allowed: 4" is the allocated capacity for reviewing-and-adjusting a plan that has already been drafted and submitted. Removing the drafting task strands this allocated planning capacity without its designated purpose, and leaves the 30-day submission window with no plan to submit, contradicting the resource plan of the process. The removal also violates Rule 3 (cost estimates required) and Rule 4 (written justification required), because these are produced by the drafting task, but the primary resource conflict is with the provisioned planning capacity described above.

Request3:
Removed element: The "Provide feedback" task (Task_3) - the step in which reviewers give feedback on the budget's feasibility after the finance department's detailed review.
Reason: It is proposed for removal to eliminate the feedback-triggered review-and-adjustment rounds and thus shorten the planning cycle, allowing the finance review to feed directly into the approval gateway.
Conflict details: This removal CONFLICTS with both rules and resources. Rules: Rule 6 of Rules.txt requires the finance department to complete its feasibility review within 15 business days, and the output of that review is communicated as feedback, and Rule 7 permits "several review-and-adjustment rounds, each documented and approved by the necessary stakeholders" - the rounds are triggered precisely when feedback indicates the plan needs adjustment. Removing the feedback step empties the review-and-adjustment mechanism of Rule 7 of its trigger, so the rounds that Rule 7 envisions cannot be initiated. Resources: the resources "Finance review deadline (business days): 15" and "Maximum review rounds allowed: 4" in Resources.txt provision the review capacity whose output is the feasibility feedback that may trigger rounds; removing the feedback task contradicts the allocated 15-day finance review and the up-to-4 review rounds that the process is resourced to perform.

Request4:
Removed element: The "Implement plan" task (Task_1) - the final step in the process in which the department begins implementing its approved plan after the budget has been distributed.
Reason: It is proposed for removal so that the process ends immediately after the budget has been distributed, under the rationale that plan implementation is an operational activity that begins after the budgeting process is over, not part of the budgeting process itself.
Conflict details: None — fully compatible with all rules and resources. No rule in Rules.txt explicitly mandates an "implement plan" step as part of the budget-planning process: Rule 9 requires budget approval, Rule 10 requires distribution after approval, Rule 11 concerns returning unspent funds at end of period, Rule 12 requires procurement not to begin until allocation is confirmed in writing, Rule 15 requires monthly spending reporting against the approved plan - but none of these rules requires a specific implementation task to be present in the model. The process description itself says "The process ends when the budget is distributed, and the department begins implementing its plan," implying that implementation follows the process, and the budgeting process properly ends with distribution. No resource in Resources.txt is tied to a plan-implementation step: resources such as "Annual department budget: $500,000", "Equipment budget allocation: $80,000", "Marketing budget allocation: $50,000", and "Hireable employees under the plan: 5" describe the contents of the budget, not the implementation activity. The process remains fully executable, so the removal does not conflict with any rule or resource.

Note (for the re-add request): Request4 removes only the "Implement plan" task (Task_1) from the model. The distribution of the budget (Task_4) is retained, and the end event is reached directly after budget distribution.
