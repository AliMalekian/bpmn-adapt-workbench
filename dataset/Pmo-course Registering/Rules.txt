1. A course can be selected only if at least one free slot is available for the intended course date.
2. The system displays available courses and dates only for courses that still have free slots.
3. Registration requires a valid account at the sports institute.
4. A new user without an account must verify registration at an eligible university before an account is created.
5. Users who are not at an eligible university must request an activation and may not register until the activation response is received.
6. Course registration cannot be completed unless payment information is provided.
7. Registration is confirmed only after payment has been successfully processed.
8. A course ticket is generated and sent only after the payment is completed.
9. Users may register for at most 3 courses per season.
10. A course is closed for registration once its maximum number of participants is reached.
11. Course cancellations are allowed with a full refund only up to 7 days before the course start date.
12. Only students currently enrolled at an eligible university quality for the student discount.
13. Completion of the registration requires the application to be connected to the user's social account to allow inviting friends.
14. Course fees must be paid in full at the time of registration; installments are not offered.
15. The activation request must be answered within 5 business days.
