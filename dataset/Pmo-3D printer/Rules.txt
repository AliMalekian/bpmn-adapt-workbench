1. The artist is given several sketches before the model project can be started.
2. Change requests are sent to the artist until the result satisfies the customer.
3. The plastic color is chosen only after the artist has delivered a satisfying project.
4. If the chosen plastic color is already at home, the remaining amount must be checked.
5. If the remaining plastic is under 100 grams, the color is put on the shopping list.
6. If the plastic color is not available at home, it is ordered.
7. The printer may only be turned on after the plastic is available.
8. The printer bed and extruder must reach operational temperature before extrusion starts; continuous telemetry status reporting during heating is an optional diagnostic feature.
9. While heating, the printer continuously sends back its status.
10. The gcode file is generated out of the STL sent by the artist.
11. Printing starts only after the gcode file is generated and the target temperatures are reached.
12. The gcode is generated only from the final, approved version of the STL.
13. The model must fit within the printer's build volume to be printable.
14. At least 100 grams of usable plastic must be loaded before a print job can start.
15. The print job is aborted automatically if the printer reports an error status.
