1. The smartphone model and the damaged part must be entered into the tool before any material or tool list is generated.
2. Materials and tools that the user already owns are marked as available at home and are not ordered.
3. A tool may be borrowed or bought from a friend only if its price is lower than the retail price plus shipping.
4. When tools or parts are sourced informally, they may be borrowed or purchased cheaply from friends; direct commercial part procurement is also fully authorized.
5. The cheapest ordering option is chosen unless its delivery time exceeds 2 weeks.
6. Repair instructions are only provided after all required materials and tools are confirmed to be in hand.
7. The repair must follow the tool's step-by-step instructions in the given order.
8. If the phone does not work after the first repair attempt, one retry is allowed before contacting an expert.
9. A review and/or repair video must be submitted to the tool after a successful repair.
10. If the repair fails after two attempts, the device is sent to an expert and no further self-repair is attempted on it.
11. Original-manufacturer replacement screens are preferred over third-party screens when the price difference is below 30%.
12. Adhesive is always ordered together with the display to guarantee proper sealing.
13. A newly glued screen must cure for at least 8 hours before the phone is powered on.
14. The tool recommends at least 3 ordering choices for each required part.
15. Screen repairs must not be performed while the phone is connected to a power source.
