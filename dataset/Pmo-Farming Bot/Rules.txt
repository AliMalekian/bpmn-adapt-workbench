1. The bot gathers only the resources that the player has explicitly selected before farming starts.
2. Up to 10 resources can be collected in parallel, and urgent resources are scheduled before less urgent ones.
3. Tools required to gather a resource must be crafted before the dependent resource can be collected.
4. Depending on the game logic, a tool's crafting dependencies must be satisfied first, otherwise the dependent resource is skipped.
5. When a random natural disaster strikes, the bot's progress is set back and collection pauses until the disaster effect expires.
6. A notification is sent to the player whenever a milestone in gathered quantity is reached.
7. The list of resources to be farmed can be updated at any time while the bot is running.
8. Resources may be sent to approved friends and displayed in a friend filter only after the bot has finished collecting.
9. Brag messages to friends are published only for milestones that are marked as shareable.
10. A natural disaster resets the gathered quantity of the affected resource by at most 20 percent.
11. The bot halts entirely if the inventory reaches its carrying capacity, and resumes after storage is cleared.
12. Tool durability below 10 percent forces a maintenance stop before further collection continues.
13. Only friends who have been approved by the player may receive materials and milestone notifications.
14. The farming scheduler checks resource priorities at most once per minute.
15. Collection of a resource stops automatically once the player-set target quantity for that resource is reached.
