1. The base airplane model is identical for every order; only the interior configuration is customized.
2. When customizing the interior, the customer selects from the offered design configurations; standard factory delivery does not mandate premium shipping arrangements.
3. The customer specifies the number of seats, their color, and the amount of toilet water in the plane.
4. All interior specifications must be confirmed by the customer before requirements are sent to the teams.
5. Each interior component type is manufactured by a dedicated team, e.g. the vodka bar by the Russian team.
6. Component teams begin manufacturing only after receiving the official requirements document.
7. The interior is assembled only after all individual bits and pieces from every team have arrived.
8. After assembly, the plane is sent on a test flight to validate the interior.
9. A test protocol is created during the flight and sent to the manufacturer and the customer.
10. The plane is delivered to the customer only after the test flight passes without non-conformities.
11. The customer must confirm receipt of the plane to close the process.
12. Seats may be produced only in the colors confirmed by the customer at order time.
13. The toilet water amount per toilet must be between 5 and 20 liters.
14. At least 5 different manufacturing teams must submit parts before assembly is authorized.
15. If the test flight reveals defects, rework is done by the responsible team and a new test flight is scheduled.
