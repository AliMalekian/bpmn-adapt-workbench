1. The basic design of the machine must be approved before any brick sets are ordered.
2. Sort containers must be labeled by part type before sorting begins.
3. Each Lego brick set must be sorted into a maximum of 12 containers.
4. A subcomponent may only be assembled using parts from the sorted containers.
5. If a container runs out of parts, individual replacement parts must be reordered before assembly continues.
6. The supply of a container may not fall below 10 parts mid-assembly without triggering a reorder.
7. Each subcomponent must pass its individual test successfully before assembly.
8. Failed subcomponents must be redesigned and rebuilt before reassembly is attempted.
9. A subcomponent may be redesigned and rebuilt at most 3 times; further failures require design approval.
10. The sorting group must contain at least 3 children working on the parts.
11. Assembly may not start until all subcomponents have passed their final tests.
12. A redesigned subcomponent must be retested completely before final assembly.
13. The finished machine may not exceed 5000 Lego parts in total.
14. Spare parts equal to 10% of the total part count must be ordered as a reserve.
15. The whole machine must be completed within 60 days of design approval.
