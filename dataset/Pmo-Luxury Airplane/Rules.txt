Rules for Luxury Airplane Interior Production

1. The customer must submit full interior specifications within 30 days of placing the order.
2. Exactly one bar must be selected from the 5 available bar options for each airplane.
3. The number of seats must not exceed 40 seats per airplane.
4. Seat color must be chosen from the 6 approved color options of the standard palette.
5. The water capacity of each toilet must be set between 2 and 25 liters.
6. Interior specifications must be confirmed in writing before requirements are sent to the manufacturing teams.
7. Manufacturing teams must deliver their parts within 45 days of receiving the requirements.
8. The vodka bar must be manufactured by the Russian team and the whiskey bar by the Irish team.
9. Every delivered part must pass a quality inspection before it is used in the interior assembly.
10. Interior assembly may begin only after all manufacturing teams have delivered and cleared the required parts.
11. The airplane may be sent on a test flight only after the complete interior is assembled.
12. A test protocol must be created during the test flight and sent to both the manufacturer and the customer.
13. The test flight must last at least 2 hours.
14. The airplane may only be delivered to the customer after the customer confirms the test protocol.
15. Any change to the approved specifications during production requires written customer approval before work continues.
