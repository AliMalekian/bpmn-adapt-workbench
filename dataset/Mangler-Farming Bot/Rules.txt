1. The player must select at least 1 and at most 10 resources to be collected in parallel by the farming bot.
2. Resources marked as urgent are farmed first, before non-urgent resources, during every bot run.
3. A tool resource must be produced before its dependent resource can be farmed.
4. Natural disasters strike randomly and set the bot's collected progress back by up to 2 days worth of resources.
5. A milestone notification is sent to the player each time 25% of a selected resource goal is reached.
6. The list of resources to be farmed can be updated at any time during the farming run without stopping the bot.
7. Bots may hold a maximum of 10 different resource types in their inventory at the same time.
8. Farming stops automatically when all selected resource goals are reached.
9. Materials can be sent to friends only when the player still has at least 50% of the collected goal left.
10. Bragging about progress is allowed only in chat channels of selected friends.
11. Each newly unlocked resource requires a minimum of one prerequisite tool to be gathered.
12. The bot can carry a maximum load of 500 units of resources per run.
13. When a disaster strikes, the bot immediately relogs the top 3 urgent resources.
14. A resource listed as "rare" requires 2 resources to be collected in a specific order before it becomes farmable.
15. The player must confirm the final collection goal list before the bot starts farming.
