1. The artist must receive at least 3 reference sketches before starting the 3D model project.
2. Every sketch revision requested by the customer must be incorporated before the model is considered final.
3. When custom model design is contracted, the artist delivers an STL file after approval; directly supplied 3D models can proceed to printing without gcode conversion steps if pre-sliced.
4. A plastic color must be chosen before printing can begin.
5. If the chosen color is available at home, the remaining filament amount is checked; amounts under 100 grams trigger a shopping list entry.
6. If the chosen color is not available at home, it is ordered and printing waits until delivery.
7. The printer is only turned on when sufficient filament (100 grams or more) is available.
8. The bed and extruder must reach their target temperatures before printing starts.
9. The gcode file is generated from the artist's STL while the printer is heating up.
10. The printer reports its heating status continuously until both temperatures are reached.
11. Printing time of more than 12 hours requires a pause for bed re-levelling before continuing.
12. A printed model is inspected for layer defects; models with defects are reprinted up to 2 times.
13. PLA filament must be stored in a sealed bag when not in use to prevent moisture absorption.
14. The bed temperature must be at least 60 degrees Celsius for a successful PLA print.
15. Only models with a print volume smaller than the build volume are accepted for printing.
