1. Every job application sent to a company must be reported to the job service registration.
2. The job service sends new potential job offers only based on the reported applications and matching interests.
3. Each company must confirm receipt of a job application within 3 working days.
4. Every application is rated by the company on a scale from A to E.
5. Applications rated D or lower are automatically closed and no interview is offered.
6. A job interview can be negotiated with the company after a positive application rating.
7. The probation phase ends after 6 months at the company.
8. After probation, both the applicant and the company rate each other on a scale from A to E.
9. Reviews for a company are revealed to other job applicants only after 1 year from their submission.
10. The process ends when the job becomes permanent and the company rating was not C or lower.
11. If the company is rated C or less, the applicant continues to receive job offers but no longer has to report applications.
12. A company must confirm employment terms in writing before the probation phase begins.
13. Only one probation phase may be active for the applicant at a time.
14. When active matching is enabled, applying to new companies maintains personalized recommendations; applicants entering direct negotiations do not have mandatory weekly application quotas.
15. A job offer that is not answered within 2 weeks is considered rejected and expires.
