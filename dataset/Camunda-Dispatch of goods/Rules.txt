1. Parcels weighing 50 kg or more must be shipped via special logistics, not regular post.
2. Shipments worth more than 1000 EUR require parcel insurance before dispatch.
3. Parcel insurance may only be arranged by the logistics department head after written approval.
4. The secretary must invite at least three logistics companies to offer for every special shipment.
5. The selected logistics offer may not exceed the cheapest valid offer by more than 5%, otherwise department head approval is required.
6. Special shipments must be handed over to the selected logistics company within 2 business days of offer acceptance.
7. End-customer parcels heavier than 30 kg are upgraded to special shipping automatically.
8. Package labels must be written before goods are handed to the mail or logistics company.
9. Standard post parcels prepared before 15:00 are dispatched on the same working day.
10. When parcels require packaging, the warehousemen must finish packaging before the logistics company arrives for pickup; bulk container shipments without packaging are permitted.
11. Special shipments to other shops valued above 5000 EUR always require special shipping and insurance.
12. Only the secretary may issue package labels; no other staff member may release parcels for shipping.
13. Logistics companies may collect goods only between 08:00 and 16:00 on business days.
14. The insured value declared for a parcel must not exceed the invoice value of the goods.
15. Special shipping costs count against the monthly logistics budget and must be recorded on the day of booking.
