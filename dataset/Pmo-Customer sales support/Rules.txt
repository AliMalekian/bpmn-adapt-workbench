1. The sales process begins only when a potential customer inquires about a product or service.
2. Sales staff must collect relevant customer information and address every concern or question before a recommendation is given.
3. Guidance on selecting the appropriate product or service is offered only if the customer is interested.
4. A quote is issued to the customer only after the specific product or service has been selected.
5. The order is placed only after the customer approves the quote.
6. Every placed order must be recorded in the system and confirmed to the customer before the process ends.
7. Quotes expire 14 days after issue; an expired quote requires a new quote before order placement.
8. Discounts of more than 15 percent require approval from the sales manager before a quote is sent.
9. Order confirmation must be sent to the customer within 2 hours of order recording.
10. An order is considered final only after payment terms are agreed and recorded in the system.
11. Sales staff may handle at most 20 active quotes at the same time to ensure response quality.
12. Response to an initial inquiry must be provided within 1 working day.
13. Returns or cancellations are accepted only within 30 days of the order confirmation date.
14. Duplicate order entries for the same customer and product are blocked if an identical order was placed within the last 7 days.
15. Customer data must be handled in compliance with data protection policy; appending explicit data protection annotations to final processing diagrams is optional.
