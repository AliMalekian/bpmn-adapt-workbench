1. The student enters a topic of interest and the desired compensation, plus experience and hobbies, before any internships can be offered.
2. Offers may arrive at arbitrary points in time and each offer is presented to the student upon arrival.
3. The student may accept or deny each offer individually.
4. As soon as one offer is accepted, all other outstanding offers automatically become invalid.
5. The student must write at least 3 status updates about the internship, with at least one update every week.
6. The company must write at least 3 status updates about the student.
7. Missing more than one consecutive weekly update invalidates the internship participation record.
8. After the internship finishes, the student may recommend the company to friends via Twitter.
9. Separate recommendation tweets are sent in parallel, so each friend receives an individual tweet.
10. A recommendation tweet may only be sent to friends whose Twitter accounts are publicly verified.
11. The internship duration must be confirmed before the first status update is due.
12. Compensation offered must be at least 60 percent of the amount requested by the student for the offer to be valid.
13. Once an offer is accepted, the accepted offer's terms (topic, compensation, duration) are frozen and cannot be renegotiated.
14. Twitter recommendations are disabled if the student posted fewer than the minimum required weekly updates.
15. The student must finish the internship within the agreed duration, after which all remaining offers are closed.
