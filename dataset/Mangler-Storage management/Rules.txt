1. Orders are read from the automatic order management system.
2. Each product of an order must be checked for stock availability before withdrawal.
3. If the product is in stock, it is withdrawn from the warehouse.
4. If the product is not in stock, it is reordered from the wholesaler.
5. Processing of an order continues only after every product has been handled.
6. If the product arrival takes longer than 10 days, a delivery delay penalty is automatically demanded from the wholesaler.
7. Arrived products must be registered in the stock management system before any further processing.
8. An order is considered ready for shipment only when all of its products have been secured.
9. Once the order is ready, a courier is requested and the products are packed.
10. Packing and the courier request are performed simultaneously.
11. The shipment happens only after the packing is finished and the courier is available.
12. If the order is not yet ready, the next product from the order is selected and the process repeats from the stock check.
13. Only one product is processed per iteration of the stock check.
14. The delay penalty is demanded automatically without manual intervention once the 10-day limit is exceeded.
15. A product that falls below its minimum stock level triggers an automatic reorder.
