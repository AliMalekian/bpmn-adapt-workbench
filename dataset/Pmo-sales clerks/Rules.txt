1. A sales clerk may request a credit score for a customer only through the software frontend after verifying the customer's identity.
2. Every scoring request is sent to the agency immediately after the frontend request is submitted.
3. Level 1 scoring is executed first and normally returns an immediate result to the banking system within seconds.
4. If level 1 cannot determine the score immediately, the agency informs the banking process of the delay.
5. When a delay notification is received, the banking process displays a message to the clerk to check again later.
6. Level 2 scoring is initiated only after the agency has reported that level 1 could not determine a result.
7. Level 2 scoring may take no longer than a couple of minutes.
8. The scoring result is displayed at the frontend as soon as it arrives from the banking process.
9. A clerk must not request scoring for the same customer more than once per hour.
10. Scoring requests are limited to 500 per day under the agency contract.
11. Customer consent for credit scoring must not be older than 90 days.
12. Clerks can view only the final score and not the intermediate agency data.
13. If no result arrives within 15 minutes, a time-out is raised and the clerk is offered the alternative manual file.
14. Scoring results are archived 6 months after being delivered to the frontend.
15. A score below 450 triggers an automatic referral to a senior loan officer for review.
