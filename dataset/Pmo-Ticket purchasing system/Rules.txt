1. A ticket is issued only after the customer provides valid personal information and completes the payment.
2. Payment must be authorized by the payment gateway before the booking is confirmed and the ticket is generated.
3. Seat inventory is updated in real time immediately after a successful booking to prevent double sales.
4. If the payment fails, the seat is released and the booking is cancelled automatically.
5. Customers are entitled to a full refund for cancellations made at least 48 hours before departure.
6. The booking system allows a maximum of 9 passengers per single reservation.
7. Tickets are sent to the customer by email or SMS within 5 minutes of a confirmed booking.
8. A customer may select only routes, dates, and times that are available in the current schedule.
9. Check-in reminders are sent 24 hours and again 3 hours before the scheduled departure.
10. Boarding is permitted only with a valid ticket and government-issued identification.
11. Seat selection is only available for tickets purchased more than 3 hours before departure.
12. Overbooking is not allowed beyond 2% of the vehicle's seat capacity for flights.
13. Post-travel feedback requests are sent within 24 hours after the journey is completed.
14. A booking may be rescheduled without a fee once, if requested more than 12 hours before departure.
15. Incomplete payments must be finalized within 10 minutes, or the held reservation is released.
