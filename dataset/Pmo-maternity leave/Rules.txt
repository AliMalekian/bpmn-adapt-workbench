1. A parent must select the maternity leave model, including the months duration and the split between parents, before any plan is finalized.
2. The total maternity leave duration must not exceed the maximum allowed by the selected model.
3. The leave split between the parents must sum to exactly 100% of the total leave entitlement.
4. The company must be notified of the leave plan at least 30 days before the leave start date.
5. Social Security must be notified of the leave plan at least 15 days before the leave start date.
6. All relevant information must be collected before the leave plan is submitted for approval.
7. Leave may start earlier than planned only with approval from both the company and Social Security.
8. At the end of the leave period, the parent may request an extension up to a maximum of 6 additional weeks.
9. An extension request must be submitted no later than 2 weeks before the end of the current leave period.
10. If the leave is split between the parents, each parent must submit a separate information form.
11. Extension approval requires confirmation from both the company and Social Security.
12. Once the leave plan is confirmed, unused leave days cannot be transferred between the parents.
13. A parent returning to work must give at least 1 week of notice before the planned return date.
14. Information gathered from companies must be validated by the employer within 10 business days.
15. If the parent's employment contract ends before the plan is confirmed, the notification to Social Security is cancelled.
