1. A dismissal may be sent by the MPON to the MPOO only after the dismissal decision has been documented and approved.
2. The MPOO must review the dismissal within 10 working days of receiving it.
3. The MPOO must respond by either opposing the dismissal or confirming the dismissal; no other outcome is permitted.
4. If the MPOO opposes the dismissal, the dismissal is returned to the MPON with a written statement of reasons.
5. A dismissal opposed by the MPOO may be resubmitted only after the stated objections have been addressed.
6. The MPOO may confirm a dismissal only if the grounds for dismissal are consistent with the applicable regulations.
7. The dismissal becomes effective no earlier than the confirmation date fixed by the MPOO.
8. Written notification of the final decision must be provided to the employee concerned in every case.
9. A dismissal confirmed by the MPOO cannot be opposed by the MPON after 30 days have elapsed.
10. Confirmed dismissals must be recorded in the personnel file within 5 working days.
11. The MPOO is not allowed to oppose or confirm a dismissal in which he or she is personally involved.
12. Each case may be reviewed at most twice before it is referred to the higher authority.
13. The dismissal process is suspended while a dispute about the grounds is pending an official ruling.
14. Reconsideration of a confirmed dismissal requires new evidence provided by the MPON.
15. All dismissal documents must be archived and retained for at least 3 years after the case is closed.
