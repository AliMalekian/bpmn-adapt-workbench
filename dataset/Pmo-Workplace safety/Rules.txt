1. Every incident report is logged into the tracking system within 24 hours of it being reported.
2. An incident is assigned to the appropriate team no later than one working day after it is logged.
3. Each assigned team must complete the investigation and root cause analysis within 14 days.
4. Corrective actions must be proposed for every incident with an identified root cause.
5. High-severity incidents must have corrective actions implemented within 5 working days.
6. A corrective action is closed only after its effectiveness has been verified during the follow-up.
7. Incident reports are closed only after the follow-up confirms the issue will not recur.
8. All stakeholders must be notified within 3 working days after the incident report is closed.
9. An incident is re-opened if a similar issue recurs within 90 days of closure.
10. Safety incidents require immediate notification to the safety officer within 2 hours of the report.
11. Training-based corrective actions must be completed by all affected employees within 30 days.
12. Technical issues are prioritized by severity, with critical issues handled within 24 hours.
13. A policy change as a corrective action must be reviewed by the compliance team before release.
14. Customer-reported incidents receive confirmation of receipt within one working day.
15. Recurring incidents of the same type are escalated to the safety committee after the third occurrence in a quarter.
