1. The service reminder process starts only after a successful car service has been completed.
2. The system must check that the car is still registered before the owner is notified.
3. Vehicles that are no longer registered are removed from the reminder process immediately.
4. The owner is fined if the required service is not performed within 30 days of the notification.
5. The fine may not exceed 200 EUR and must be validated by an officer before issue.
6. The mechanic must enter the reported car problems for reference before the repair starts.
7. The owner must receive a status update by email every 2 days while the service is open.
8. Payment through the app is accepted only after the repair is marked as successful by the mechanic.
9. The "Pickerl" sticker may be issued only after the repair has passed inspection.
10. The mechanic must enter the date for the next service at the end of the repair process.
11. The next service date may not be set later than the legally required 12-month interval.
12. A service left open for more than 7 days must be escalated to the facility manager.
13. The owner must be notified of the imposed fine no later than 7 days after the 30-day deadline.
14. Payment for a completed service must be settled within 14 days of receiving the invoice.
15. The repair record including problems, updates, and next-service date must be archived for 5 years.
