1. A dismissal may be sent by the MSPN to the MSPO only after the termination decision is formally recorded.
2. The MSPO must review each dismissal within 10 working days of receipt.
3. The MSPO must either reject or confirm the dismissal; no other outcome is permitted.
4. If the MSPO rejects the dismissal, it is returned to the MSPN together with the rejection reasons.
5. A rejected dismissal may be resubmitted only after the MSPN addresses the stated reasons for rejection.
6. The MSPO may confirm a dismissal only when the legal grounds for termination are verified as valid.
7. A dismissal takes effect only after the effective date decided by the MSPO is reached.
8. The affected person must be notified in writing of the final decision in every case.
9. The MSPN may not re-submit the same dismissal more than twice before the MSPO refers the case to the higher authority.
10. Confirmed dismissals must be entered in the personnel records within 5 working days.
11. The MSPO cannot review a dismissal in which he or she has a personal interest.
12. Dismissal proceedings are halted while a related legal appeal is pending.
13. A previously confirmed dismissal can be reconsidered only on the basis of newly discovered evidence.
14. Terms of separation stated in the confirmation must be executed by the MSPN without alteration.
15. All dismissal documentation must be kept in the archive for at least 5 years.
