1. The next service reminder is scheduled only after a successful previous service has been recorded in the system.
2. Before notifying the car owner, the system must verify that the car is still registered.
3. If the car is not registered, no service reminder is sent and the process is suspended until registration is verified.
4. If the owner does not bring the car to the service within 30 days after notification, a fine is issued automatically by the police app.
5. The mechanic must record all reported problems with the car, which become part of the reference history for the repair.
6. The owner receives status updates by email at least once every 2 days while a repair lasts longer than one day.
7. Payment via the app is enabled only after the mechanic marks the repair as successfully completed.
8. The "Pickerl" (safety inspection sticker) is issued only after the repair and the technical inspection are both successful.
9. The mechanic must set the next service date, which defaults to 12 months after the current successful service.
10. A repair that exceeds 7 working days requires the service facility to send an additional detailed progress notification.
11. The fine for a missed service may be issued only once per reminder cycle; a new 30-day countdown starts after each reminder.
12. The car invoice must be fully settled through the app before the Pickerl is released to the owner.
13. The reminder process restarts only after the owner has completed the service and the mechanic confirms it.
14. The service facility may accept at most 8 incoming cars per day for full service to guarantee inspection quality.
15. If a car has gone more than 2 years without a recorded service, a mandatory technical inspection must be booked before a regular service appointment is confirmed.
