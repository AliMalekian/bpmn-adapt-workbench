Rules for Inventory Restocking in a Warehouse or Store
4. When evaluating competitive new tenders, supplier selection takes into account cost, delivery time, and supplier reliability; repeat standard catalogue orders do not require re-evaluation.
1. Inventory levels must be monitored in the system at least twice a day.
2. When stock for an item falls below its predefined reorder threshold, an automated alert is triggered.
3. Only authorized procurement staff may place orders with suppliers.
4. Supplier selection must take into account cost, delivery time, and supplier reliability.
5. The inventory system must be updated with expected delivery dates within 24 hours of placing an order.
6. Delivered stock must be inspected for quality before it is recorded in the system.
7. Stock that fails quality inspection is rejected and returned to the supplier at the supplier's expense.
8. Accepted stock must be recorded in the inventory system within 8 hours of delivery.
9. Stock may be placed on shelves or in storage only after it has been recorded in the system.
10. The standard reorder threshold for critical items is 20 units.
11. Manual inventory checks must be confirmed by a second staff member before any reorder is triggered.
12. A supplier with a delivery time longer than 14 days must be reviewed before being used again.
13. The minimum order quantity accepted from any supplier is 50 units.
14. Discrepancies between delivered quantity and ordered quantity must be resolved within 2 days of delivery.
15. The process ends only after the inventory levels are updated following the complete restock.
