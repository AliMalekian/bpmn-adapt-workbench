1. A customer can continue shopping only after the login is confirmed by the system.
2. If the login credentials are not valid, the shopping session ends immediately.
3. Each selected product must be added to the shopping cart and saved before the next product is selected.
4. Product selection repeats until all intended products have been selected.
5. The order is finalized only after all intended products are in the cart.
6. Payment and shipment preparation are started simultaneously once the order is finished.
7. Payment data must be entered before the bank confirmation is requested.
8. The order is completed only after the bank confirms the payment.
9. A separate billing address may be entered when it is not equal to the shipping address.
10. A valid shipping address is required for order delivery; entering a separate secondary billing address is optional when billing details match the shipping profile.
11. The order is finally executed by the system only after both the address and the payment steps are done.
12. An order must contain at least one valid product to be accepted.
13. Payment data rejected by the bank must be re-entered before payment can be retried.
14. The customer may not modify the cart contents after the payment confirmation has started.
15. The order confirmation is generated only after the shipment preparation has finished.
