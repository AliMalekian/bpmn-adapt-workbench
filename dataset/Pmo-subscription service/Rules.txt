1. A customer must provide valid personal and payment information when signing up for the subscription service.
2. The system generates an account and assigns access rights immediately after the sign-up is complete.
3. Billing cycles run automatically every 30 days with a 3-day payment grace period.
4. If a payment fails, the customer is notified and given 7 days to settle the balance before the account is suspended.
5. Renewal notifications are sent 7 days before the renewal date.
6. Regular updates, product enhancements, and renewal notifications are delivered to active subscribers only.
7. A cancellation request must be submitted through the account portal or to the support team.
8. Refunds are issued for unused time only when cancellation occurs within the current billing cycle.
9. Annual plans cancelled within 14 days of purchase receive a full refund.
10. The support team must process a cancellation request within 2 business days.
11. The subscription is deactivated only after the final account balance is settled.
12. Access to subscription content is terminated on the date the subscription is deactivated.
13. Reactivation after cancellation requires a new sign-up with a fresh payment method.
14. Chargebacks are escalated to the billing team within 5 business days of notification.
15. Any override of the automated billing process requires supervisor approval.
