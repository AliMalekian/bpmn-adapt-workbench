1. The procedure may begin only after the implements are sterilized, the hands are washed, and the doctor is in sterile clothing.
2. The ultrasound probe must be covered and sterile gel applied before it contacts the patient.
3. The vein is identified using at least one of the three accepted methods: anatomic, doppler, or compression identification.
4. If the blood return is not correct, the doctor must repeat the puncture step before proceeding.
5. If the wire is not in the good position, the doctor must go back to the puncture step.
6. The puncture attempt may be repeated no more than 3 times per insertion site.
7. Sterile gloves must be replaced immediately if their sterility is compromised at any point during the procedure.
8. The catheter may be advanced only after the guidewire is confirmed to be in the correct position.
9. The guidewire is removed only after the catheter has been advanced into position.
10. The doctor verifies flow and reflow, and checks the catheter position, before the procedure is declared complete.
11. Patient vitals must be monitored continuously from anesthesia administration until the procedure is finished.
12. Ultrasound gel must be applied only to the probe after it is properly configured and positioned.
13. A maximum of 2 skin puncture attempts is permitted before the doctor must consider an alternative insertion site.
14. All used needles, guidewires, and trocars must be disposed of in the sharps container immediately after use.
15. The procedure requires the presence of a circulating nurse throughout the entire intervention.
