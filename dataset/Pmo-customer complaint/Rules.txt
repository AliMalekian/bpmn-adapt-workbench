1. A complaint can be logged only after it is filed by the customer through an accepted channel (phone, email, or web form).
2. Customer service must assign the complaint to the relevant department within 1 business day of logging it.
3. Every complaint is investigated and the customer notified of the decision within 14 business days.
4. A complaint is considered valid only if the investigation confirms that a defective product or failed service is responsible.
5. A refund is justified only when the product is returned in its original condition or the service failure is confirmed by records.
6. Refund eligibility is limited to purchases made within the last 60 days.
7. Refunds above $1,000 require the approval of the department manager.
8. The reimbursement is processed by the financial team within 5 business days after refund approval.
9. A complaint is marked as resolved only after the customer receives the refund or after the rejection is notified.
10. Customers whose refund is rejected may appeal the decision within 14 days of notification.
11. Complaints concerning health or safety issues prevent the product from being restocked until the review is complete.
12. A complaint that is answered by the same department that created the problem must be escalated one level.
13. Customer satisfaction feedback is collected after every resolved case.
14. A complaint that receives no reply within 10 business days is automatically escalated to a supervisor.
15. Repeat complaints about the same product defect trigger a mandatory root-cause analysis for each occurrence.
