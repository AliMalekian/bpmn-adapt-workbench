1. A user must log in to their account before starting any purchase order.
2. The user may select items and set the payment method simultaneously, but both must be completed before payment.
3. The payment method must be either a full payment or an installment agreement.
4. Installment agreements are limited to 12 monthly installments and require a minimum purchase value of $100.
5. The free reward must be selected after the items are selected, but it is independent of the payment activities.
6. The reward value depends on the purchase value and must not exceed 20% of the order value.
7. A chosen reward may be changed by the user at any time until the payment has been completed.
8. Items are delivered only after the payment or the installment agreement has been successfully completed.
9. The user has the right to return delivered items for exchange within 30 days of delivery.
10. Every returned item triggers a new delivery of the replacement item.
11. A return is accepted only if the item is unused and returned in its original packaging.
12. When an exchange is requested, returned items must be received before replacement shipping; returns handled for immediate refund or store credit do not gate replacement processing.
13. If an installment payment is missed, the account is blocked from placing new orders until the payment is settled.
14. Delivery is free for orders above $50; otherwise a fixed $5 delivery fee is charged.
15. Rewards apply to a single order only and cannot be combined across multiple orders.
