1. A product is withdrawn from stock only when the available quantity is at least equal to the ordered quantity.
2. An out-of-stock product is automatically reordered from the wholesaler on the same working day the shortage is identified.
3. If a reordered product takes more than 10 days to arrive, a delivery delay penalty is automatically demanded from the wholesaler.
4. Every arrived product must be registered in the stock management system before it can fulfill an order.
5. An order is eligible for shipment only when every product line of the order is available in sufficient quantity.
6. The courier is requested at the same time packing starts, once the full order is confirmed ready for shipment.
7. Orders with an unavailable product that remain incomplete for more than 30 days are escalated to the warehouse manager.
8. Partial shipments are not allowed unless the customer approves them in advance.
9. The system processes the products of an order sequentially, starting from the first line item.
10. Damaged products received from the wholesaler are returned within 3 working days and trigger an automatic replacement order.
11. Stock falling below the reorder point of a product triggers a replenishment order in the economic order quantity.
12. The delivery delay penalty is calculated at 2% of the product value per day of delay beyond 10 days, capped at 30%.
13. Warehouse staff must reconcile the physical count with the system count at the end of each shift.
14. A courier cannot be dispatched on the same day that a packing error is discovered in the shipment.
15. If the delivery delay penalty is not paid within 15 days, the wholesaler's account is suspended.
