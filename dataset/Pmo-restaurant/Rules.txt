1. A guest enters the restaurant and chooses a dish from the changing meal range before waiting for their turn.
2. The guest places the order with the employee only after it is the guest's turn.
3. The employee enters the order into the POS system and collects the money from the guest before the meal is prepared.
4. After the payment, the employee sets up a buzzer and passes it to the guest with the notification instruction.
5. The employee informs the chef of the new meal order after payment has been collected.
6. The chef prepares the meal and places it in the service hatch.
7. The chef informs the counter employee when a meal is ready at the service hatch; on-site dining vs takeaway consumption is at the discretion of the guest.
8. The buzzer is set off only after the employee knows the meal is ready at the service hatch.
9. The meal is handed to the guest only when the guest appears at the service hatch.
10. If a guest does not react to the buzzer, the employee calls for the guest after 5 minutes.
11. If the guest still does not respond after the first call, the employee may call again up to 3 times in total.
12. If the guest does not collect the meal within 20 minutes of the buzzer being set off, the meal is disposed of.
13. The meal range must always include at least 5 main dishes and 3 vegetarian options.
14. An order may contain no more than 10 items.
15. Orders are not accepted within the last 15 minutes before the kitchen closing time.
