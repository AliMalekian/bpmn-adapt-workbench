1. An application is only considered complete when all required documents have been submitted; incomplete applications are placed on hold for no more than 10 business days.
2. The application fee must be paid in full before the admissions committee evaluates the application; fee waivers are granted only upon proof of financial need.
3. The admissions committee must notify applicants of a decision within 4 weeks of receiving a complete application.
4. Applicants must meet the minimum admission GPA of 2.5 (or its international equivalent) to be eligible for evaluation.
5. International applicants must have their previous academic credentials evaluated before the visa process can begin.
6. A student must confirm enrollment within 2 weeks of receiving the acceptance letter, otherwise the application is canceled.
7. First-year students must meet with an academic advisor before selecting any courses.
8. Full-time status requires registration for at least 12 credits and at most 18 credits per semester.
9. Schedule conflicts must be resolved before final course registration is approved; no more than 2 timetable overlaps are allowed without advisor authorization.
10. Courses may be added or dropped only within the add/drop period of the first 2 weeks of each semester.
11. A student must be registered for at least 12 credits to remain eligible for scholarships and financial aid.
12. A grade appeal must be filed no later than 30 days after grades are posted.
13. The appeals committee must issue a decision within 3 weeks of receiving the appeal form.
14. If a student requests a physical ID card, it is issued only after enrollment confirmation is finalized and any outstanding fees are paid; digital credentials do not require physical card issuance.
15. Registration beyond 18 credits requires the approval of the academic advisor and a minimum GPA of 3.0.
