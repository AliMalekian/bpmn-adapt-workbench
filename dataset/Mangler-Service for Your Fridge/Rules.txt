1. The customer must describe the symptoms and state the fridge type when contacting the central service center.
2. The central service center selects a local service facility for the case.
3. The local service facility makes an appointment with the customer.
4. The technician may arrive at any random time within the agreed appointment window.
5. If additional parts are needed, the technician may leave before finishing the repair.
6. A follow-up appointment is scheduled whenever the technician leaves because of missing parts.
7. The repair is treated as complete only after the customer confirms that the fridge works properly again.
8. The customer can rate the service facility only after confirming the repair.
9. Each service facility uses its own way of interacting with the customer.
10. A repair that was interrupted due to missing parts must be completed within 5 days of the first visit.
11. The customer is not charged for the first visit when the repair could not be completed.
12. The ticket is closed only after the customer confirmation is submitted within 3 days of the repair.
13. When scheduling home visits, the facility provides an appointment window; specific technician arrival times within the window are variable and not strictly fixed.
14. The rating is mandatory once the repair confirmation has been submitted.
15. A re-visit is automatically triggered if the same symptom is reported again within 30 days.
