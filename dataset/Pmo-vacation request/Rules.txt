1. An employee must submit a vacation request at least 3 working days before the intended start date.
2. Only employees who have completed the probation period of 6 months are eligible to request paid vacation.
3. The immediate supervisor must approve or reject a vacation request within 2 working days of receiving it.
4. A vacation request is automatically rejected if the employee has already used the maximum annual leave entitlement of 20 days.
5. No more than 2 employees from the same team may be on vacation at the same time.
6. Vacation requests are not approved during peak production periods unless the department manager approves them in advance.
7. A single vacation block cannot exceed 10 consecutive working days; longer periods require senior manager approval.
8. Vacation days must be used within the same calendar year; no more than 5 days may be carried over to the next year.
9. Paid vacation accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per month of full-time service.
10. After a rejection, the employee may submit a revised request no earlier than 3 working days after receiving the rejection notification.
11. An approved vacation must be recorded in the HR system within 1 working day of the HR representative receiving the notification.
12. An employee cannot take vacation during weeks in which they are scheduled for mandatory training or an internal audit.
13. An emergency vacation request may be submitted on the day of departure but requires approval from both the supervisor and the HR representative.
14. Vacation of more than 5 consecutive days requires an approved handover plan for the employee's responsibilities.
15. An employee may have at most 1 pending vacation request at any time.
