1. An accident is classified as a work accident only if it occurs at a location, at a time, and with a cause that correlates to the insured employment.
2. Accidents on the direct commuting route between home and work, including carpools, are covered.
3. A doctor's visit is covered only if it interrupts the direct work route and was notified to the employer in advance.
4. Child transport trips to or from a childcare facility or school are covered when the insured has supervisory responsibility for the child.
5. Students and schoolchildren must report work accidents to the competent directorate of their institution.
6. An employee must inform the employer immediately of any work accident, near-accident, serious safety risk, or defect in protection systems.
7. The employer must immediately report any work accident causing a fatality or serious injury to the Labour Inspectorate if it was not already reported to emergency services.
8. Every work accident that kills an insured person or leaves them unable to work for at least three days must be reported to the accident insurance provider within five days.
9. Schools, educational facilities, and universities must report every covered accident in triplicate to the insurance provider within five days.
10. In private insurance, accidents must be reported in writing immediately, and fatalities within three days, even if the accident was already reported.
11. Accidents during volunteer service for recognized aid organizations are treated as work accidents even outside professional activity.
12. Self-employed persons must report accidents involving both their employees and themselves to the insurance provider in good time.
13. Agricultural and forestry establishment accidents are covered even when they do not occur directly during insured employment, within the listed scope.
14. An accident is not covered if the insured acted contrary to a residence prohibition or an alert issued by a Schengen country applies.
15. Assistance provided at an accident scene, or while donating blood, is covered as work accident activity.
