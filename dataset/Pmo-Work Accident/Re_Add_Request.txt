Restored element: The "Inform relatives" task — the step where the relatives of an injured or deceased insured person are informed about the work accident and its consequences.

Detailed restoration: Request4 removed the step where the relatives of the affected person are informed. To restore the process to its original state, the "Inform relatives" step must be re-added as follows:

1. When a work accident occurs and the process determines that the person was injured (and, in the case of a fatal accident, has died), the process again performs the "Inform relatives" task.
2. During "Inform relatives," the employer (or the responsible party in the process) contacts the family members/relatives of the injured or deceased person and informs them about the accident, the person's condition, and the next steps in the claims and care process.
3. In the death branch of the process, the relatives are informed in parallel with the formal notifications — the process again runs "Inform relatives," "Notify insurance" (Rule 8: within 5 days for a fatal accident), and, where applicable, "Notify labor inspectorate" (Rule 7: immediate for fatality or serious injury) and "Notify insurance about the death" (per Rule 8 and the resource "Fatal accident reporting deadline (days): 3").
4. The process continues with the task "Stay informed about the person's condition" — the involved parties, including the family, remain updated on the insured person's medical situation until the issue is resolved.

This restores the complete original process: accident occurs, type of event determined, person insured checked, employer informed (Rule 6), death/serious injury assessed, Labour Inspectorate notified (Rule 7), insurance notified (Rule 8), relatives informed, person's condition monitored, issues fixed and resolved.
