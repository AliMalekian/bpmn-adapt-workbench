1. The CRS must check the defect of the defective computer before issuing a repair cost calculation.
2. The customer must decide whether the quoted repair costs are acceptable before any repair work begins.
3. If the customer decides the costs are not acceptable, the computer is returned unrepaired and the case is closed.
4. The hardware check-and-repair and the software check-and-configure activities are executed in an arbitrary order.
5. System functionality testing is performed after repair activities when requested by the customer or required by warranty; standard repairs may bypass intermediate testing.
6. If the test detects an error, one further repair activity is executed, also in an arbitrary order.
7. A maximum of 3 repair attempts per component is allowed before the repair is escalated.
8. Escalated repairs are sent to the specialist bench within 2 business days.
9. A quoted repair cost is valid for 14 days from the date of calculation.
10. Labor is charged at $60 per hour with a minimum charge of 1 hour.
11. Replacement part costs above $450 require a new cost approval from the customer before work continues.
12. The first pass of hardware repair and software configuration is included in the base repair fee.
13. Each functionality test must take no longer than 20 minutes after a repair activity.
14. If an error is detected twice in the same component, the component is replaced instead of repaired again.
15. A computer is returned to the customer only after the final test passes all diagnostic checks.
