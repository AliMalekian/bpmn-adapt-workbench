Rules for DIY Repair of a Broken Smartphone Screen

1. The user must enter the exact phone model and the reported damage before any guidance is provided.
2. The tool must generate a list of required materials and a list of tools for the entered model.
3. Several ordering choices must be shown for each required part.
4. Tools and parts the user already owns are marked as available and excluded from purchase.
5. Friends may be contacted to borrow or buy parts only through the tool's sharing feature.
6. Parts not covered by owned stock or friends must be purchased from approved suppliers.
7. Step-by-step repair instructions are provided only after all parts and tools are secured.
8. Only parts and tools compatible with the entered model are recommended.
9. The repair must be performed with the device powered off and the battery disconnected.
10. The user must confirm each completed repair step before proceeding to the next instruction.
11. If the repair succeeds, the user is asked to send a review or a video of the repair.
12. If the repair has not worked out, the user can send the phone to a certified expert.
13. Expert repair support must be requested within 14 days of the DIY attempt.
14. Replacement screens must carry a 12-month warranty.
15. User-submitted reviews and videos are published only after passing a quality check.
