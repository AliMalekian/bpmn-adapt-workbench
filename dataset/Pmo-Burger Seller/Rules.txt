1. If the customer selects a menu, the drink must be prepared first before the robot asks whether the customer wants fries or wedges.
2. A customer may choose either fries or wedges, but not both in a single order.
3. Drink, fries, wedges, and burger preparation durations vary depending on the selected size and the number of burger ingredients.
4. The robot sends an enthusiastic status update to the customer every 30 seconds while the burger is being prepared.
5. An order is released onto the conveyor belt only after the burger, the side, and the drink (if a menu) are all fully prepared.
6. During peak lunch hours (11:30 to 14:00), the order queue is limited to 15 concurrent customer orders.
7. A new order is accepted only if the stock of every required ingredient is at least 2 portions above its reorder minimum.
8. Fried items (fries or wedges) must be served fresh; no batch may remain in the warming tray longer than 10 minutes.
9. The robot processes at most 3 burger preparation runs in parallel to guarantee consistent cooking quality.
10. A burger-only order may not include a drink unless the customer explicitly adds one; menus always include a drink.
11. The conveyor belt may hold a maximum of 5 completed orders at once before it must be cleared for delivery.
12. Payment must be completed before preparation starts; prepaid orders receive priority in the queue.
13. Allergen information (nuts, gluten, lactose) must be displayed on the ordering screen for every menu item.
14. If two identical orders arrive within 60 seconds, the robot batches them into a single preparation run.
15. New orders are not accepted within the last 5 minutes before closing so that the cleaning cycle can be completed.
