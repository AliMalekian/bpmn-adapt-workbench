1. The requirements list must be complete and signed by the owner before it is sent to the architect.
2. The architect must deliver the first draft within 3 weeks of receiving the requirements.
3. Each refinement round with additional requirements must be answered with a revised draft within 1 week.
4. Refinement rounds are limited to 5 rounds before a final plan must be frozen.
5. The final materials list must be created only from the approved architectural plan.
6. Materials are ordered from online stores only after the final plan is approved.
7. Orders exceeding 2000 EUR from a single store require owner approval.
8. Friends are asked to help build the house only after all material orders have been placed.
9. At least 3 friends must be available to start the construction phase.
10. The tree house may not exceed a platform height of 4 meters.
11. A party invitation is sent only after the house is fully built and inspected.
12. Snack quantities are based on the number of attendees on the party list.
13. The snack budget may not exceed 15 EUR per attending person.
14. The total construction budget, including materials and snacks, may not exceed 15000 EUR.
15. The house must be completed within 6 months of the plan approval.
