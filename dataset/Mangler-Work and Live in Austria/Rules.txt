1. The travel document must stay valid for at least three months beyond the duration of the visa.
2. The travel document must contain at least two empty pages.
3. The travel document must have been issued within the last ten years.
4. A passport photo in portrait format 35 x 45 mm meeting the specified criteria must be provided.
5. A fully comprehensive travel health insurance policy valid for the whole Schengen area with a cover of 30,000 euro is required.
6. The applicant must prove sufficient means of subsistence for the intended stay and for the return journey.
7. Additional evidence such as hotel reservations, invitations, booking confirmations, or a return flight ticket may be requested depending on local standards.
8. The application is refused if there is a residence prohibition or an alert issued by a Schengen country.
9. Visas are issued by Austrian representation offices abroad, or in exceptional cases by border control posts.
10. A Schengen visa must be issued by the representation office of the country of the main travel destination.
11. If no main travel destination can be determined, the member country of first entry into the Schengen area issues the visa.
12. Category A and C visa applications fall under the representation office of the consular district of the applicant's legal residence.
13. Type D visas must be applied for at the competent Austrian representation office.
14. Territorial competence is based on the foreign national's centre of interest, verifiable by registration forms, residence permit, or visa.
15. A repeated visa is required every x months and is processed under the Rot-Weiss-Rot Card scheme.
