1. A guest must pay for the meal at the till before the order can be entered into the POS system.
2. The buzzer is activated only after the POS system confirms the guest's payment.
3. The employee must trigger the buzzer within 1 minute after the chef reports the meal as ready.
4. If a guest does not react to the buzzer, the employee calls for them after 5 minutes.
5. Calls for uncollected meals are repeated at 5-minute intervals, up to 3 times in a row.
6. A meal left in the service hatch for more than 10 minutes is discarded and re-prepared only on request.
7. The chef prepares meals strictly in the order of POS entry.
8. The chef places each finished meal in the service hatch and immediately informs the employee.
9. Each guest may have only one open order at a time; the next order is accepted after the previous meal is collected.
10. The daily meal range must offer at least 6 dishes, including one vegetarian and one gluten-free option.
11. The service hatch may contain at most 10 finished meals at the same time.
12. Cash payments above 50 EUR must be verified by a second employee before acceptance.
13. Buzzer units with a battery level below 20% must not be handed to guests.
14. The restaurant is limited to 120 active buzzers at any moment.
15. Guests who miss three buzzer calls and do not appear within 30 minutes lose their meal and payment claim.
