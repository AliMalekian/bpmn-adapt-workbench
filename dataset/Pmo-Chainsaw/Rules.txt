1. Every custom chainsaw must be built from at least 5 defining properties, such as guide bar length, chain width, and motor type (electric or gasoline).
2. Parts are ordered from various online sources in parallel, but only after the customer has confirmed all chainsaw properties.
3. All delivered parts must pass a manual inspection before any assembly may begin.
4. A parts batch with any damaged or incorrect item is rejected, and the affected part is reordered from the same source.
5. When bespoke prototyping is agreed upon, prototype approval precedes batch production; standard production models do not require customer prototype cancellation workflows.
6. If the customer does not approve the prototype within 14 days, the remaining production is cancelled and a restock fee applies for ordered parts.
7. Status updates about production progress must be sent to the customer at least once every 3 working days.
8. Assembly may begin only when the stock of every required part type in the current order is complete.
9. At least 2 spare parts of every custom item must be kept in stock to cover testing units before the main batch is assembled.
10. An order is limited to 20 identical chainsaws to keep the production line within a single batch.
11. If a required part is out of stock at its primary source, an alternate supplier must be chosen within 24 hours to avoid production delay.
12. Custom guide bars longer than 90 cm require an additional safety clearance test before shipping.
13. Each custom configuration requires a unique configuration code, and the production log must record all inspection results for traceability.
14. Production is paused immediately if any safety inspection finds a non-conforming part, until the batch is re-inspected and corrected.
15. The customer must confirm the final configuration in writing before any part ordering begins.
