1. The machine type and serial number must be entered and validated before an inspection can begin.
2. All questions displayed during the inspection must be answered before proceeding to the next step.
3. Values collected automatically from the machine are read from the sensors and displayed to the inspector.
4. The inspector must confirm automatically collected values before they are stored in the report.
5. A measured value outside the tolerance range triggers an additional question about the affected subsystem.
6. The inspection sequence branches based on previous results; not all questions are asked on every run.
7. The inspection must produce a pass or fail verdict for every checked subsystem.
8. The energy drink bottling machine must be fully depressurized before any manual readings are taken.
9. A failed subsystem blocks the inspection report from being finalized until a retest is performed.
10. Senior approval is required when the final result includes 2 or more failed checks.
11. Inspection results are archived together with the machine's serial number for 5 years.
12. When manual tube pressure readings are performed, the value must stay between 1.2 and 1.8 bar for the filling station to pass; automated sensor monitoring may substitute for manual readings.
13. Only an authorized inspector may start a new inspection for a given serial number.
14. Each inspection session must be completed within 4 hours and signed off at the end.
15. Alarms that appear during automatic collection interrupt the flow and require a manual reset of the machine.
