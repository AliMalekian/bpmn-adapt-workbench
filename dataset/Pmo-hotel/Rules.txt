1. The room-service manager accepts room-service orders from guests only during the operating hours of the room-service station.
2. Every order ticket is submitted to the kitchen to begin food preparation as soon as the order is taken.
3. A separate order is given to the sommelier whenever the order includes wine or an alcoholic beverage.
4. A room-service order is assigned to a waiter only after the order has been taken in full.
5. The waiter may wait to bill a guest if he has another order to prepare or deliver; the older order is billed first.
6. The waiter is responsible for preparing and serving all nonalcoholic drinks in the order.
7. The room-service cart must carry a proper tablecloth and full silverware set before delivery.
8. The order is delivered to the guest's room only when the food, the wine, and the cart are all ready.
9. Alcoholic beverages are served only to guests who can present valid identification proving they are of legal drinking age.
10. The guest's account is debited after delivery and after the waiter returns to the room-service station.
11. Alcoholic beverages are not left unattended, but are served only on arrival at the guest's room.
12. Room-service stations allow at most 20 concurrent orders in the kitchen queue per shift.
13. Holds on guest accounts are released only after the full room-service bill is debited.
14. Food and beverages must be delivered to the guest room within 45 minutes of the order being taken.
15. The sommelier verifies the cellar stock before confirming an order for a wine that is nearly depleted.
